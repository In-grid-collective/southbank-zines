Hosted on the Raspberry Pi on-site in Southbank Centre, this page presents a list of zines by artists and organisations that are part of the Creative Intelligence Exhibition, available for download. Each zine is a 2-sided, printable PDF file in A3.

PING is an installation and a piece of artist made network infrastructure by In-grid. The structure holds a series of small board computers and screens, which are servers connected through a Virtual Private Network (VPN) hosting ping.in-grid.io in the space. Traffic from visitors' mobile devices will influence the installation's screen based visuals, revealing the connections and messages sent between the devices and visitors. The work exposes the standard information shared between web browsers and servers, while redacting sensitive details, inviting visitors to reflect on everyday mechanisms of trust online.
