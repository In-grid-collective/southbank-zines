PING!

PING is a mutable, modular installation and a piece of artist made network infrastructure. It is both sculptural object, and the structure allowing us to serve you up this website. Only when PING is fully installed is this website accessible.

The structure holds a series of small board computers and screens, which are servers connected through a Virtual Private Network (VPN) hosting ping.in-grid.io in the exhibition spaces. It posits a version of infrastructure that is tangible, material and idiosyncratic.

You can interact with the work by visiting the site ping.in-grid.io to see this record of the work so far. Traffic via visitors’ mobile devices will influence the installation’s screen based visuals. Visitors can see how web traffic travels through our network of servers as it travels to the computer hosting PING in the space. The work exposes the standard information shared between web browsers and servers, while redacting sensitive details, inviting visitors to reflect on everyday mechanisms of being online.

PING was first commissioned by Peckham Digital for the Get Your Hands Dirty with Creative Tech section of the Southbank Centre’s Creative Intelligence festival in 2026.
